431: ---Who is eligible for Free Legal Aid--- As per chapter VI, Rule 9, of the Delhi Legal Services Authority Regulation 2002 following person are eligible for free Legal Aid Services. 
432: SC or ST 
433: Victim of trafficking or begar 
434: Women or Child 
435: Person with disabilities 
436: Victim of Mass Disaster/Ethnic Violence Caste Atrocity/Flood/ Earthquake or Industrial Disaster 
437: Industrial Workmen 
438: In Custody, including custody in Protective Home/ Juvenile Home/Psychiatric Hospital/ Psychiatric Nursing Home 
439: Person having annual income less than Rs. 3 Lac 
440: Senior Citizen having annual income less than Rs. 4 Lac 
441: Transgender having annual income less than Rs. 4 Lac 
442: Acid Attack Victim 
443: Person infected and affected with HIV Aids 
447: Help Line: 15100 & 1516 (24*7), 9870101337 
448: Email : lae-dslsa@gov.in
